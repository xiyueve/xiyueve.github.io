About Me

Hello! My name is Eve, and I am currently a second-year mechanical engineering student at the University of Wisconsin, Madison. My primary interests lie in instrumentation and electromechanical systems. I hope to develop instruments for planetary science research in the future.

In my free time, I enjoy reading science fiction novels and taking photos while hiking. I also like listening to classical music and have a particular inclination towards Tchaikovsky and Bach.

Relevant Coursework: Linear Algebra, Differential Equations, Multivariable Calculus, Materials Science, Statics, CAD

Skills/Software: SolidWorks, Onshape, Python, Java, HTML, CSS, MATLAB, Git, Laser Cutting, 3D Printing, Arduino Programming, Introductory GD&T

Global Languages: Chinese (Native), Spanish (Upper-Intermediate), Russian (Novice)

Awards: Dean's List (all semesters), Strategic Targeted Achievement Recognition Scholar (for high-achieving students within the College of Engineering), William Holberg Scholarship (in recognition of outstanding academic performance within the Department of Mechanical Engineering)
